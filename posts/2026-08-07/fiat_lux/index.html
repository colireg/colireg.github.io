FIAT LUX

it took me many months to figure out what am i gonna do with this website. the dust was slowly piling up on its source code in corner of my hard drive. i bet there were some spiders considering moving there too which i'm glad they didn't (even though i'm a bug enthusiast, i don't appreciate them in software).

i had plans to add more sections to the website for things that aren't either a post or a photo. i wanted to write generator scripts that update each page of this website so i wouldn't need to manually copy/paste <nav> each time i update it. i also wanted a separate section for silly stuff i make or cool things i discover about html and css. it was taking too long and i was worrying there won't be enough energy left to host this website when i finish it after the heat death of the universe so i decided to tidy things up and publish it as it is and then slowly change it overtime.

i have a lot of ideas for this blog and i have no idea which one of them i'll actually bring into reality. all i know right now is that there will be posts about tech stuff, linguistics and even posts in weird constructed languages once in a while.

so here it is.
